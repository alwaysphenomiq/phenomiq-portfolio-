Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Phenomiq is a web design and development studio in Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria. This page explains what we collect when you use phenomiq.com.ng and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • The details you type into our contact form: your name, your phone number or email, and your message.
  • Anything you send us directly by WhatsApp, phone call or email.
  • Your light or dark mode choice, saved only in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you hire us, to carry out the work. We do not sell your details, rent them out or use them for advertising.

Where it is stored

Contact form messages are stored securely in a database that only Phenomiq can access, and we receive an email notification when a new message arrives. The website itself is hosted on Vercel. We keep enquiries for as long as they are useful to follow up, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Third-party content

The map in the contact section is provided by Google and may set its own cookies when it loads. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will respond within a reasonable time. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Contact

Email info@phenomiq.com.ng or message us on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab).
