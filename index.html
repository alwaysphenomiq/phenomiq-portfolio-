Questions people ask.

Short answers before you message us.

How long does it take to build my website?

Most business websites go live in 7 to 10 days. We agree the pages, the scope and the price in writing before work starts, so you know the timeline from day one.

Do you work with businesses outside Abeokuta?

Yes. Phenomiq is based in Abeokuta, Ogun State, and builds websites for businesses across Nigeria. We work with you by WhatsApp, phone call and email, so you do not need to travel.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Most of your customers will find you on their phone, so every site is designed for a phone screen first and then scaled up for tablets and computers. Pages are built to load fast on mobile data.

Can customers book or pay on my website?

Yes. We can add booking and registration forms, online payment with Paystack or Flutterwave, automatic receipts and a simple page where you manage every request and order.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. A store can include a product catalogue, cart and checkout, or simple ordering by WhatsApp, plus an orders page you can manage.

Will my business show up on Google?

We build every site with clean structure, fast loading and the right titles, descriptions and business details, so Google can understand what you do and where you work. No one can honestly promise a number one ranking, but a well built site gives you the best start.
